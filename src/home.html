What you'll build

Every one of these is a real model that learns in front of you, from random numbers, on your own device.

  • Teach a neuron a ruleShow it examples, watch it nudge its own weights until it gets every one right.
  • Crack the XOR puzzleOne neuron can't solve it. Add a hidden layer and the impossible becomes easy.
  • Step through backpropagationFollow the blame backwards through a network, one real number at a time.
  • Invent brand-new namesTrain a language model that makes up names, one letter at a time.
  • Sort 10,000 headlinesBuild a model that reads real news and decides what each story is about.
  • Read your handwritingTrain a digit reader on thousands of real scribbles, then draw for it.
  • Make pictures from noiseThe trick behind AI image generators, small enough to watch working.
  • Learn a maze by trial and errorNo right answers given, only rewards. Watch a creature work out the way.

Who it's for

Curious 13-year-olds reading on their own, and anyone older who never got a straight answer about how this works. The first chapter covers the handful of math symbols you need.

Private by design

Every network in this book trains inside your browser. Nothing you type, draw or train is sent anywhere, and your progress is saved only on this device.

Chapters with Python fetch a code editor and the Python engine from public code libraries. Your code still runs on your own device.
